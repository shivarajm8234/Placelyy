Interview Preparation Notes

Placelyy · Prep · Sample document

Use this page as a template for your own interview prep notes. Swap in company-specific questions and resources as needed.

Technical focus

  • Data structures & algorithms — arrays, trees, graphs, DP
  • Core CS — OS, DBMS, networks, OOP
  • Projects — architecture, trade-offs, your role

Behavioural prompts

  • Tell me about a conflict on a team and how you resolved it
  • Describe a time you failed and what you learned
  • Why this company / role?

Day-of checklist

  • Stable internet, quiet space, charged device
  • Résumé and question list open nearby
  • Arrive 10 minutes early for virtual or on-campus rounds
