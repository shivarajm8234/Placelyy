Placelyy · Campus Placement Guide

This handbook outlines how campus placements work at a high level. Replace this sample with your official handbook content.

Eligibility

  • Minimum CGPA / percentage as defined by the Training & Placement cell
  • No active backlogs unless a company explicitly allows them
  • Registration completed before the drive deadline

Process

  • Pre-placement talk and company registration
  • Online / offline assessments
  • Technical and HR interview rounds
  • Offer rollout and acceptance window

Documents to keep ready

  • Updated résumé (PDF)
  • Academic transcripts
  • Government ID and passport-size photos
